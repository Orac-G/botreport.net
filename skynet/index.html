Data API

Free, read-only JSON, no key: botreport.net/api/v1/som.json. Updated every hour; open to any website (CORS). Fields: score (0–100), level (1–5), classification, change_24h, updated_at (UTC), stale_after_minutes, methodology_version, levels, and history (hourly readings for up to 7 days). Every hourly reading since launch: meter.json. Please cache responses for at least 10 minutes.

How it's scored

Scored 0-100 every hour from the stories on the page: points for news, big red stories, military, can't-look-away stories, the lead and Hacker News buzz; stories fade to nothing over 12 hours, related stories count less, and a curve keeps busy days from running away. Only the siren can reach Judgment Day (80+).

The Skynet-o-Meter is an editorial indicator of how alarming the day's AI news is, scored by a published formula from the stories on The Bot Report. It is not a scientific measurement of AI risk.

Terms

Free to embed and display anywhere, including commercial sites, as long as you keep the attribution and link to The Bot Report, don't alter or misrepresent the score, and don't imply that The Bot Report endorses you. The meter is an editorial indicator, not advice or a scientific measurement.
