    What the levels mean · how it's scored

      The score is worked out fresh at every update from the stories on the page. Each story from the last 24 hours earns points: 1 for being news, +5 if it's a big red story, +3 for military, +2 for a can't-look-away story, +3 for the lead and +1 per 1,000 Hacker News comments (up to 3). Stories count in full for 3 hours, then fade steadily to nothing after a day. When several stories belong to one saga, the biggest counts in full, the second at half and the rest at a quarter. The total goes on a curve that starts at 10 and flattens out below 80. The siren adds 20, and the editor can move it up or down by as much as 10. Only the siren can take it to Judgment Day; otherwise it stops at 79. Recalibrated on Nov. 24, 2026, so earlier scores ran higher and aren't comparable.
